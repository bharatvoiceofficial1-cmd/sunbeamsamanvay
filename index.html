About The Conclave

Where Ideas Collide & Leadership Begins

Samanvay is a flagship Youth Parliament and Model United Nations conference hosted by Sunbeam Academy, Samneghat. Its inaugural edition convenes students from across India to debate public issues, question established paradigms, and take part in the conversations that matter.

Through parliamentary debate and international diplomacy, delegates test arguments, listen to opposing views, and understand the geopolitical complexities shaping India and the world.

1. Building Arguments

Through research, empirical facts, and a clear understanding of legal and policy issues.

2. Parliamentary Engagement

Experiencing the real workings of legislative debate and international negotiations.

3. Space for Dialogue

Bringing together students with different viewpoints to engage, question, and respond.

“रुक गए तो औरों का कदम बन जाओ, जो थक गए तो राह का दीया बन जाओ।”

— A Note from the Sunbeam Family & Secretariat

Conclave Structure

Two Arenas of Consequential Discourse

Delegates can choose between domestic parliamentary simulations governing India’s legal frontiers, or global multilateral chambers addressing international security.

Chamber 01

Indian Youth Parliament

Simulating the Indian Parliament and legislative assemblies. Master bill drafting, constitutional defense, zero-hour interventions, and cross-party consensus on national policies.

Lok Sabha: FCRA Amendment Bill 2026 & National Security
AIPPM: Simultaneous Polls (One Nation, One Election) & AI Deepfakes
UPLA: UP State Employment Strategy & Exam Transparency
Janmanch: School Education Reforms & NEP 2020 (Classes VI–X)
View Parliamentary Agendas
Chamber 02

Model United Nations

Step into the roles of international ambassadors and press journalists. Deliberate international treaties, resolve humanitarian crises, and protect civil liberties under the UN Charter.

UNHRC: State Surveillance, Pegasus Spyware & Biometric Privacy
UNCSW: Women's Economic Empowerment & Workplace Legal Reforms
UNGA: Reforming Multilateral Institutions & Geopolitics
IIP: International Indian Press, Media Blackouts & Free Speech
View Diplomatic Agendas
